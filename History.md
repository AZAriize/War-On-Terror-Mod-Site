# History of the War on Terror
A simple overview of the major events that shaped the War on Terror. This page provides context for players without going into sensitive or graphic detail.

## 1. Background (Before 2001)
In the late 20th century, several regions experienced rising instability due to:
- political tensions
- regional conflicts
- extremist groups gaining influence
- shifting international alliances

These conditions shaped the environment leading into the early 2000s.

## 2. 2001 – The War Begins
A series of major terrorist attacks in 2001 triggered a global response. The United States and allied nations launched operations aimed at:
- disrupting extremist networks
- removing supportive regimes
- preventing future attacks

This marked the official start of the War on Terror.

## 3. 2001–2002 – Afghanistan Operations
Coalition forces entered Afghanistan with goals to:
- target extremist leadership
- dismantle training infrastructure
- support the formation of a new government

This became one of the longest international missions of the era.

## 4. 2003 – Iraq Invasion
In 2003, the United States and coalition partners launched operations in Iraq. Their objectives included:
- removing the existing government
- addressing concerns about weapons programs
- reshaping regional politics

This opened a second major front in the broader conflict.

## 5. 2004–2010 – Insurgency and Regional Shifts
During this period:
- insurgent groups expanded
- coalition forces adapted strategies
- regional governments faced internal challenges
- international alliances shifted

This era was defined by long, complex operations and political transitions.

## 6. 2011–2014 – New Movements and Power Vacuums
As governments changed and foreign forces reduced their presence, new groups emerged. This period saw:
- political uprisings
- regional instability
- the rise of new extremist organizations

These developments reshaped the conflict dramatically.

## 7. 2014–2020 – Global Coalition Efforts
A broad international coalition formed to counter newly emerging extremist groups. Key themes of this era include:
- multinational military operations
- regional partnerships
- stabilization efforts

By the late 2010s, many major groups had lost territory, though tensions remained.

## 8. 2020 and Beyond – Aftermath and Ongoing Challenges
Even after major operations ended, the War on Terror left lasting effects:
- political transitions
- regional rivalries
- counterterrorism cooperation
- reconstruction efforts

Your mod explores this timeline in a grounded, historical way.
